# Sourdough Notes

## Feeding the starter

Feed the starter once a day at room temperature, or twice a day if the kitchen is warm. A reliable ratio is 1:2:2 — one part starter, two parts flour, two parts water by weight. Kept in the fridge, the starter only needs feeding once a week. Discard all but a spoonful before feeding so the culture does not become too acidic.

## Bulk fermentation

After mixing, let the dough rise at room temperature until it has grown by roughly half and shows bubbles on the surface. This bulk fermentation usually takes three to five hours depending on temperature. Fold the dough gently every 30 to 45 minutes during the first two hours to build strength without degassing it.

## Baking schedule

Bake in a Dutch oven at 245°C: 20 minutes with the lid on so steam keeps the crust soft while the loaf expands, then 20 to 25 minutes with the lid off for a deep brown crust. Score the top with a lame just before baking so the loaf opens along the cut instead of tearing. Let the bread cool at least two hours before slicing, or the crumb turns gummy.
